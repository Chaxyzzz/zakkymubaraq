## About

I am **Zakky Mubaraq**, Founder of **ATLAS Studio**.

I enjoy building software that is clean, scalable, and designed with purpose. My work combines software engineering with visual creativity, allowing me to create digital products that are both functional and intuitive.

Beyond programming, I explore photography, videography, graphic design, and digital media. These disciplines influence the way I think about user experience, product design, and problem solving.

For me, technology is not only about writing code—it is about creating meaningful experiences that people can use, understand, and enjoy.

---

## Philosophy

Great products are rarely built by complexity.

They are built by clarity.

I believe good software should feel effortless to use. Every interface should have a purpose, every feature should solve a real problem, and every line of code should contribute to something meaningful.

Rather than chasing trends, I focus on building products that are maintainable, reliable, and valuable over the long term.

---

## Principles

- Simplicity over complexity.
- Quality over quantity.
- Consistency over shortcuts.
- Learning never stops.
- Design and engineering should work together.
- Every project deserves thoughtful attention.
